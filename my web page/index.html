Popular Destinations

Ella

Explore beautiful mountains, waterfalls and the famous Nine Arch Bridge.

Galle

Discover the historic Galle Fort and enjoy beautiful coastal views.

Sigiriya

Visit the ancient Sigiriya Rock Fortress and discover Sri Lanka's history.

Kandy

Experience Sri Lankan culture and visit the famous Temple of the Tooth.

Mirissa

Relax on tropical beaches and enjoy exciting whale watching tours.

Nuwara Eliya

Enjoy cool weather, tea plantations and beautiful green landscapes.

Why Visit Sri Lanka?

  • Beautiful tropical beaches
  • Majestic mountains and waterfalls
  • Rich historical culture
  • Wildlife and national parks
  • Delicious Sri Lankan food
